MilkyWish

What is MilkyWish?
MilkyWish lets you send wishes to the stars. Name a star, write your wish, draw on it, and release it into a shared sky visible to everyone. Each star lives for a chosen time — from a single day to a whole year — before it fades away.
Frequently Asked Questions
Why does MilkyWish need my location?
Location is used to place your star on the Wish Map at the right spot in the sky for your part of the world. It is only accessed while the app is open — never in the background.
Can I delete a star I published?
Stars expire automatically at the end of their chosen lifetime. If you need a star removed sooner, email us at mykytaiosdeveloper@gmail.com and we will delete it manually.
Is my personal information stored?
No. MilkyWish never asks for your name, email, or Apple ID. You are assigned a random anonymous ID — nothing that identifies you personally is stored.
Stars are not loading — what should I do?
Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, delete and reinstall MilkyWish. If it still occurs, please contact us.
How do I delete all my data?
Delete the app to remove your local cache. To also remove your published stars and anonymous account from our servers, email us and we will handle it within 30 days. You can also visit our Privacy Choices page to submit a request.
